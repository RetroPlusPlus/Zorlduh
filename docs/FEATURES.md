# Features

One row per feature. A feature's document, when it has one, is `docs/features/<name>.md`, named as in
the first column. Status changes are logged in `docs/features/CHANGELOG.md`.

**Status:** ⬜ planned · 🟡 in progress · 🧪 testing · ✅ complete · ❌ dropped

| Feature | Status | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| build-system | ✅ complete | CMake with `add_subdirectory(engine)`; `zorlduh-lib`, the `zorlduh` executable, one GoogleTest executable per test file; a Release-by-default, dead-stripped, symbol-stripped binary that opens a 60 Hz Game Boy Color window |
| layer-kit | 🧪 testing | The return types (with `Raster`), `LayerOptions` and `RasterLayerOptions`, and one aggregate per kind of layer content — `SpriteLayer`, `TileLayer`, `RasterLayer`, `ImageLayer`: a header-only module in `modules/LayerKit/` that depends on the engine alone, with its own guide (`modules/LayerKit/docs/`) and a compiled getting-started program |
| assets | 🧪 testing | Placeholder art written by `tools/placeholder_art.py` under `assets/` — 2-bit grayscale index sheets (the overlay, sprite, hearts and glyph sheets' index 0 transparent), palette images, and viewable grayscale tile and collision maps, one pair per area, with a map for each further layer of an area; the glyph sheet of 43 uppercase characters and its text and shadow palettes; the wood and stone border sheets and their palettes; the sign and rock terrain tiles; 61 files — and `zorlduh::loadAssets`, which uploads every sheet and palette, decodes every area's maps and builds the game's font, all baked into the executable |
| world | ❌ dropped | One world map of free-form rooms with a room table, `zorlduh::World` and `WorldTiles`; replaced by one map per area in `world-kit` and `areas`. The depth table and the screen layout it added (`src/render/layers.h`, `layout.h`) stay |
| top-down-movement | 🧪 testing | `TopDownMovement` in `modules/TopDownMovement/`: a `WalkerConfig` (speed, collision shape, anchors, a walk animation per facing), a `Walker` that `walk` moves a tick — a pixel at a time per axis, stopping flush at walls and carrying on along one walked into diagonally — with the facing law and the walk animation, collision shapes from boxes, engine shapes or anchors against a `TileCollision` map or any query, `readDirections`, and `WalkerSprite`; header-only, depending on the engine alone, with its own guide and a compiled getting-started program |
| player | 🧪 testing | The player as a walker: `PlayerConfig` from the player sheet's four rows, the action map (arrows, WASD, d-pad; attack, item, start), `GameState`, `Config` and `tick`, and the `Player` component, drawn at each area's actor slot between what lies underfoot and what hangs overhead |
| text-kit | 🧪 testing | `TextKit` in `modules/TextKit/`: a `Font` (a glyph sheet, the character of each slot as a string, a palette, an optional shadow palette, the pen's step) with the case rule — a character drawn by its own glyph or else its other case's — and `glyphOf`; `Text`, a passage as sprites, one per glyph, keyed by character position, laid out in lines, aligned left, center or right within a width, with a shadow sprite beneath each glyph, throwing for a character the font lacks; `wrap` and `measure`; and `Reveal`, a typewriter advanced once per tick; header-only, depending on the engine alone, with its own guide and a compiled getting-started program over a font sheet and palettes of its own |
| area-name | 🧪 testing | The HUD strip's first content: the current area's name (`areaName`), right-aligned across the strip in the game's font with its shadow, typed out by a `Reveal` in `GameState` that starts over on entering an area (`AreaName`, on its own sprite layer at the HUD's depth) |
| window-kit | 🧪 testing | `WindowKit` in `modules/WindowKit/`: a `Border` (a sheet of the nine pieces of a box and an arrow, and its palette), `thickness` and `inside`; `Box`, a rectangle of any pixel size filled and framed by pieces of any size, each piece past the end of its span cut by a region of its own, keyed by its place in the box and the box's size; `Window`, the box beneath a game's sprites on one sprite layer; `Dialog`, a message whose box opens and closes, wrapped and paged to its room, typed out, with a blinking arrow, and a confirm that finishes whatever is in progress; `DialogText`, `DialogArrow` and `DialogBox`; header-only on the engine, LayerKit and TextKit, with its own guide and a compiled getting-started program over art of its own |
| window-animation | 🧪 testing | Windows that open and close over time, in WindowKit: `Animation`, how far open a window is, over a time or a count of ticks along an easing curve, reversible and skippable; five styles drawn from it — `Grow` from any point, `Clip`, `Squash`, `Fade`, `Slide` — with `Style` and `Animate`; a `Window` that takes an animation and shows its contents once open; a `Dialog` that runs its own box's animation |
| signs | 🧪 testing | Things in the world that are read: a wooden sign in the meadow and a stone rock beside the lake's dungeon door, each a solid cell, read with Item while facing it (`src/game/signs.{h,cpp}`); the message's box opens and closes over 300 ms, growing from its middle for those two, and in the shrine six signs on a grid each open theirs in one of the six styles, saying which; the world holds still while the message is up, and Item finishes whatever the message is doing — the box opening, the page typing, the page, the box closing; the message box (`Dialog`, `src/render/dialog.{h,cpp}`) across the bottom of the screen above the HUD, in the border of what is read |
| camera-kit | 🧪 testing | `CameraKit` in `modules/CameraKit/`: a `View` (the screen the camera shows and where it sits on a layer), a `Camera` value with `place`, a straight `pan` over N ticks through the engine's tween, and `scroll` for a view, and the `follow` law — center the focus, stop at the bounds' edges, center bounds smaller than the view; header-only, depending on the engine alone, with its own guide and a compiled getting-started program |
| world-kit | 🧪 testing | `WorldKit` in `modules/WorldKit/`: a `World` of `Layout`s of `Area`s — each area its collision map, its `MapLayer`s (tile maps each with its own art catalog and depth), its named `ActorSlot` depths, and named `Opening`s in walls or at spots, each opening with its `Transition` (scroll, fade or seamless) and where it `leadsTo`, in any layout, with an optional cell offset — checked by `validate`; a `Camera` that hands out the area's solid query (reaching into the next area past a seam), follows a box by the layout's camera law only as the box moves, slides the next area in along the door's axis, fades to the entrance, or switches across a seam with nothing moving, and hands back the move to apply; `WorldLayers` (a tile layer per map layer of each area shown, at its depth, confined to the view), `Actors` (the game's sprites at the current area's slot), `TopZ` and `Fade`; areas drawn through chains of seams, as many seams out as the layout's `seamDepth` allows; header-only on the engine, LayerKit and CameraKit, with its own guide and a compiled getting-started program |
| areas | 🧪 testing | The game's world of three layouts: the overworld of five areas (meadow, lake, field, shrine, cave), each its own map pair, joined by scrolling doors and a fading cave mouth, the lake's door down into `dungeon-one`, and the field's forest path down into `seamless-forest` (`src/game/areas.{h,cpp}`); the player goes between them through the tick, which records `AreaChanged` with the layout and area; every area a ground layer and the `"actors"` slot at depths from the game's depth table, the meadow's cave mouth under rock; the screen is the areas' layers with the actors among them and the fade, under the HUD strip |
| dungeon-one | 🧪 testing | The dungeon layout: five rooms (entrance, hall, west wing, east wing, treasury) with their own art, entered by a fade from the lake; doors that scroll between the entrance, the hall and the wings; the hall's whole north wall open to the treasury as a seam |
| seamless-forest | 🧪 testing | The forest layout: seven rooms (edge, glade, thicket, bower, hollow, brook, clearing) of different sizes, joined only by seams, each pair a case of how the camera follows — equal to the view, bigger, smaller with two seams, seen through another room, a chain shorter than the view, a join two cells lower, a room entered off center — with its own tile sheet and grass and leaf palettes, entered by a scroll from the field; a path under the player's feet in the edge and a band of canopy over the player in the glade |
| actors | ⬜ planned | Stable actor ids, per-area spawn tables, one wandering enemy |
| combat | ⬜ planned | The sword, enemy damage and death, player damage, invulnerability, death and restart |
| hud-and-pickup | ⬜ planned | The hearts on the HUD and the heart pickup |
| sound-cues | ⬜ planned | One sound per game event, from the event stream |
